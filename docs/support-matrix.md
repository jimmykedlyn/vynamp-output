## Levels

- `capture-verified`: delivered bytes compared bit for bit at a point that includes the OS path.
- `hardware-verified`: a manual entry in [hardware-matrix.md](hardware-matrix.md).
- `integration`: opened, played, drained and lost on the real OS stack in CI.
- `library-verified`: proven by the Null and Capture hosts in every CI run.
- `compiled`: the target builds; nothing was run (macOS stays here until a Mac agent exists).
